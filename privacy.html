Data Protection & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • GDPR & Maritime Data Protection Compliant

01. Introduction & Data Protection Roles

At Taneet (operated by Mozuk Marine), we recognize the sensitive nature of commercial fleet records, statutory vessel documentation, and seafarer personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, encrypt, and safeguard information across taneet.net and the Taneet Maritime Operating Platform.

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and international maritime data privacy frameworks:

  • Mozuk Marine as Data Controller: When you visit our website, request a demo, or register a commercial fleet account, Mozuk Marine acts as the Data Controller for your business contact and billing details.
  • Mozuk Marine as Data Processor: When a shipowner, fleet manager, or crewing agency ("Customer") uploads seafarer documentation, crew certificates, or vessel logs into Taneet, the Customer acts as the Data Controller and Mozuk Marine acts strictly as the Data Processor acting on the Customer's instructions.

02. Information We Collect & Process

We collect and process only the categories of data required to deliver secure fleet operations:

Account & Fleet Admin Data

Company name, IMO company identification, administrator names, corporate email addresses, role permissions, and billing records.

Seafarer & Crew Records

Processed on behalf of Customers: seafarer names, ranks, nationalities, STCW certificates, medical fitness certificates, passports, Seaman's Books, and next-of-kin contact details.

Vessel & Operational Records

Statutory and class certificates, survey reports, deck and engine log book entries, officer sign-offs, and onboard spare part inventories.

Security & Audit Trail Logs

IP addresses, login timestamps, document upload/modification audit trails, and browser session tokens for maritime compliance verification.

03. How We Use Information

We use collected data strictly for the following operational purposes:

  • Platform Operation: Hosting, indexing, and synchronizing vessel certificates, crew files, digital ship log books, and spare part inventories across shore offices and vessels.
  • Automated Smart Alerts: Calculating document validity windows and dispatching automated email and in-app notifications prior to certificate or crew document expiration.
  • Audit & Inspection Readiness: Maintaining tamper-evident timestamps and officer sign-off logs for Flag State, Port State Control (PSC), and ISM audits.
  • Security & Support: Preventing unauthorized access, troubleshooting synchronization issues, and providing customer onboarding assistance.

04. Encryption & Maritime Data Security

Protecting your fleet and seafarer data is core to our architecture:

  • Encryption in Transit & at Rest: All data transmitted between vessels, shore offices, and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.3 (HTTPS), and all stored documents and databases are encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption.
  • Tenant Isolation & Role-Based Access: Each Customer's fleet workspace is logically isolated. Granular role-based access controls ensure that personnel only view vessels or modules authorized by their Fleet Administrator.
  • Zero Commercial Monetization: Mozuk Marine never sells, rents, or trades Customer fleet data or seafarer personal information to third parties or advertisers.

05. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosures

We only share data under the following limited circumstances:

  • Customer-Authorized Maritime Authorities: When explicitly initiated by authorized Customer personnel (for example, exporting or sharing a certificate package with Port State Control, Flag State inspectors, class surveyors, or charterers).
  • Vetted Cloud Infrastructure Sub-Processors: Trusted cloud hosting, encrypted storage, and transactional email delivery providers bound by strict GDPR-compliant Data Processing Agreements (DPAs).
  • Legal Obligations: Where required by applicable law, court order, or binding maritime regulatory inquiry.

06. Cookies & Session Technologies

Taneet uses essential cookies and local storage tokens to maintain secure authenticated sessions, remember your cookie consent preferences, and support offline-first caching aboard vessels with intermittent connectivity. Optional analytics cookies are only activated if you select "Accept All" on our Cookie & Privacy Notice banner, and you may opt out at any time by selecting "Essential Only".

07. Data Retention & Your Privacy Rights

We retain Customer fleet and crew data for the duration of the active subscription agreement. Upon account termination or written request from the Customer, we provide full data export followed by permanent deletion of all Customer records from active production systems within 30 days (and from encrypted disaster-recovery backups within 90 days).

Individuals and seafarers have the right to request access, correction, portability, or deletion of their personal data. Seafarers whose records are managed by a shipping company using Taneet should direct requests to their employing shipping company (the Data Controller), or contact us at info@mozuk.net and we will promptly assist the relevant Customer in fulfilling the request.
