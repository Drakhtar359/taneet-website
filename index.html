Taneet Core Modules

Taneet centralizes vessel records, certifications, personnel documentation, and operational logs through dedicated functional modules.

Module 01

Technical & Non-Technical Certificates

Maintains an indexed digital archive for all vessel documentation. Covers technical certificates (classification surveys, statutory certificates, machinery manuals, safety equipment) as well as non-technical documents (commercial insurance, charter documents, and flag state registry records).

  • Digital repository for statutory certificates (SEC, Load Line, IOPP, ISM/ISPS)
  • Non-technical documents: P&I insurance, charter party docs, and commercial records
  • Proactive 90, 60, and 30-day alerts before any certificate expires
Module 02

Crew Documentation & Certification

Provides complete tracking for all seafarers onboard and in reserve. Stores and monitors Certificates of Competency (CoC), STCW endorsements, national seaman books, passports, medical fitness certificates, and contract start/end dates.

  • Rank-specific documentation hierarchy (Master, Chief Engineer, Officers, Deck Ratings)
  • Continuous tracking of seafarer medical validity, visas, and passport dates
  • Instant crew list generation with Port State Control compliance verification
Module 03

Digital Ship Log Book

Modernizes traditional paper log entries into a secure, searchable electronic log book. Records deck operations, engine milestones, maintenance entries, bunkering logs, and official ship events with time stamps and author attribution.

  • Structured electronic entries for deck, engine room, and safety events
  • Inspection-ready digital log history accessible for audits and superintendent reviews
  • Reduced transcription errors and streamlined ship-to-shore reporting
Module 04

Spare Part Inventory Tracking

Tracks all spare parts onboard across the vessel, whether technical components, ship replacement parts, or general equipment stores. Every item is systematically indexed with its exact onboard location, part number, and associated machinery for instant lookup and inventory control.

  • Indexed onboard storage locations (store rooms, engine room, shelves, and lockers)
  • Detailed tracking by spare part number, specifications, and available stock levels
  • Direct mapping of spare parts to their designated shipboard machinery and systems

Frequently Asked Questions

Tracking in Taneet is not done through hardware AIS integrations. Instead, Taneet connects to your vessels through trusted external tracking websites (such as MarineTraffic and VesselFinder) via direct vessel links. This allows you to check current positions, navigational status, and voyages immediately alongside all your vessel documents without requiring expensive hardware setups.
Taneet tracks both technical documents (Classification Society survey records, Statutory certificates like Load Line, Safety Equipment, IOPP, ISM/ISPS, and machinery specifications) and non-technical documents (commercial insurance policies, charter party documentation, flag registrations, and administrative port documents), complete with active validity dates and automated expiration warnings.
Taneet maintains comprehensive crew files organized by rank. It monitors seafarer Certificates of Competency (CoC), STCW endorsements, national seaman service books, passports, medical clearance certificates, and employment contracts. The platform alerts managers in advance so renewals are completed well ahead of crew changes.
The Digital Log Book replaces physical paper logs with a clean, structured electronic journal. It allows ship and shore staff to record and search operational events, engine room entries, maintenance activities, and safety drills, keeping a clean and organized digital history for inspections.
You can easily onboard your fleet records, crew lists, and existing certificate archives. Our structured setup ensures that all your technical files, certificates, and crew data are categorized, indexed, and accessible within days.
