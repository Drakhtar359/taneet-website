System Architecture

How the Taneet Platform Functions

Taneet bridges vessel shipboards, crewing headquarters, technical superintendents, and commercial owners through four tightly integrated functional subsystems.

Subsystem 01

Fleet Operations & Live AIS Telemetry

Provides complete situational awareness across commercial shipping fleets. Integrates satellite AIS positional feeds with onboard GPS telemetry to track voyage trajectory, Speed Over Ground (SOG), Course Over Ground (COG), heading, and precise estimated port arrival times.

  • Continuous satellite and terrestrial vessel tracking
  • Real-time operational statuses: At Sea, In Port, Bunkering, Drydock
  • Historical voyage track playback and fuel consumption correlation
Subsystem 02

Automated STCW Crew & Manning Compliance

Eliminates administrative overhead and Port State Control (PSC) deficiency exposure. Manages seafarer profiles, rank hierarchies (Master, Chief Engineer, Officers, Deck Ratings), service days onboard, and automated certificate validity tracking.

  • Automated verification of Certificates of Competency (CoC) and flag endorsements
  • 90-60-30 day proactive alerts before passport, medical, or STCW expiry
  • Seamless crew relief scheduling and minimum safe manning validation
Subsystem 03

Class Surveys & Statutory Inspections

Maintains full lifecycle visibility over classification society audits and flag state statutory certificates. Tracks Special Surveys, Intermediate Surveys, Annual Hull & Machinery inspections, Load Line certificates, and ISM/ISPS safety code compliance.

  • Survey renewal windows and condition of class tracking
  • Scheduled drydock yard project specifications and milestone logs
  • Centralized audit-ready digital certificates and surveyor reports
Subsystem 04

Vessel Registry & Owner Transparency Portals

Houses complete master particulars for every vessel—dimensions, deadweight tonnage (DWT), main engine specifications, ballast water treatment systems (BWTS), and auxiliary generators. Enables dedicated client portals for ship owners and charterers.

  • Role-gated portal allowing owners to review vessel status and performance
  • Fuel index benchmarks and speed efficiency curves
  • Instant export of vessel particulars, Q88, and commercial specs
